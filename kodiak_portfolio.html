How I Worked

A repeatable 5-step process applied at scale

Step 01

Signal Hunt

Scanned LinkedIn, company posts, role changes, and launches for one recent, observable signal per contact.

Step 02

Pain Synthesis

Translated the signal into the most likely execution-level pain, grounded in the contact's role and company moment.

Step 03

Angle Selection

Assigned Insight, Validation, or Question based on seniority, signal strength, and Kodiak fit.

Step 04

Email Draft

Three-paragraph structure: signal observation, peer-framed pain, Kodiak positioning with a soft specific CTA.

Step 05

Quality Pass

Removed AI phrasing, generic openers, em-dashes, and filler. Every email reviewed against tone benchmarks.

Craft Standards

Non-negotiable rules applied to every email

No Contractions

Maintains a professional register appropriate for VP to CRO audiences throughout every email.

No Em-Dashes

A Kodiak-specific tone marker. Prose kept clean and unambiguous when scanned quickly.

Signal-Grounded Openers

Every opener references something recent and observable — a post, announcement, or promotion. Never generic praise.

Peer-Framed Pain

Pain positioned as "in my experience" observations. The contact never feels criticised or talked down to.

Soft CTA

"Compare notes" and "worth a quick conversation" — low-stakes language, not pushy calendar requests.

Human-Sounding Copy

No buzzwords, no AI affirmations, no corporate filler. Reads like a smart peer wrote it.

Brief Kodiak Mention

Kodiak appears once in paragraph three — a logical next step, never the focus of the email.

Specific Subject Lines

Subject lines referencing the real context of the email. No manufactured urgency, no clickbait.
